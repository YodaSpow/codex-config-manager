# Heartbeat

**Version:** 1.0 · **Updated:** 16 September 2026

## Purpose

Keep repository-level `AGENTS.md` a compact entrance and hazard map for fresh agents: what the project is, what is true and authorised now, where specialist detail lives, and which predictable repository-specific mistakes to avoid. Preserve history without making agents load it all.

Fresh tasks consume the approved map; evidence-rich ongoing work may produce a beat after an outcome settles. Repeatability belongs to the method, not the snapshot: unchanged evidence produces the same material result—normally **aligned**—rather than invented churn.

## Boundaries

- Govern only the active repository's `AGENTS.md`; never global guidance or global skill policy.
- Run on demand, never by timer, chat length, activity, document count, or automatic edit.
- Treat chat as candidate evidence, not durable authority. Put lasting uncaptured decisions in canonical project documentation before routing fresh agents to them.
- Removing material from mandatory focus does not delete or rewrite its source. Preserve provenance unless separately authorised.
- Do not create a Heartbeat log; current guidance, canonical sources, and version history preserve prior pulses.

## Trigger and drift

Run on demand. Otherwise, evidence-rich ongoing work may suggest one review at a natural completion boundary, after the user has absorbed an accepted, settled outcome and evidence indicates that the entrance map changed. Fresh tasks do not produce beats by consuming the map. Name the outcome and suspected routing impact, then ask once; a decline or deferral closes the prompt.

Settled outcomes include validated success, accepted rejection, stopped or incomplete work, resolved blockers, decisions, and completed goal or document lifecycles. A later review compares the previous map with all accepted evidence and needs no missed-beat ledger. A repository may optionally carry one concise rule permitting this suggestion; it is never self-invoking and passes normal review and approval gates.

Classify handoff risk:

- **Aligned** — fresh agents can find current intent, authority, state, routes, and critical safeguards. Make no change.
- **Tolerable drift** — differences would not materially change how a fresh agent works. Make no change.
- **Material drift** — guidance exposes obsolete authority, hides a governing route or recurring hazard, or leaves accepted truth trapped in chat. Recommend a review.

## Evidence contract

Classify material by present function, not age or document number:

- **Intent spine** — the narrowest coherent route explaining why the project exists and what it owns.
- **Durable foundation** — identity, invariants, ownership, and safety boundaries that survive milestones.
- **Current state** — the latest verified implementation, schema, production state, or handoff evidence.
- **Execution authority** — the active goal, approved plan, or explicit user instruction; documentation alone does not activate work.
- **Task route** — specialist contracts and protocols needed only for relevant work.
- **Historical evidence** — completed, superseded, rejected, or intermediate material retained for provenance.

Intent may remain durable while procedures change. Active goals authorise bounded work; completed goals retain evidence, not authority. A completion report may be the best current handoff until a living contract incorporates it, but is never permanently mandatory.

Code proves implementation, not policy; documentation records intent or authority, not implementation proof. Triangulate documents, goals, code, tests, and accepted outcomes. If verified reality lacks a current documentary owner, report a documentation-parity gap; never present obsolete procedure as current or invent policy from code.

## Workflow

### 1. Audit the previous pulse

Read repository `AGENTS.md` as the previous heartbeat. Inspect documentation and goal indexes, intent sources, active goals, current-state evidence, relevant implementation and tests, and version history only where it explains the map.

For each material entry, establish its claim, landed or prospective state, supporting or superseding evidence, and disposition: **retain**, **rewrite**, **reroute**, or **remove from mandatory focus**. Supersession may be partial. User authorship requires careful reassessment, not permanent retention: keep it while evidence or unresolved human intent supports it; otherwise recommend its disposition, asking only when evidence cannot decide.

### 2. Build the entrance and hazard map

Choose the smallest always-read baseline exposing project intent, irreversible invariants, standing safety boundaries, current authority, and latest verified material state. Route a multi-document intent spine through its narrowest entry point, not every origin document.

Assign each source one reading obligation:

- **Mandatory baseline** — the smallest ordered set; read every selected source completely before project decisions, never substituting headings or search snippets.
- **Conditional route** — state the task trigger; read the selected source completely before acting in that area.
- **Historical source** — keep it discoverable for provenance, outside the startup path.

Use executable reading instructions, not passive links. If complete mandatory reading is too broad, select a narrower current entry source. Canonical documents own detailed contracts; `AGENTS.md` carries concise rules and pointers.

Expose an active goal or prepared decision when it is current authority, but never select or activate the next goal through routing alone.

### 3. Apply the admission gate

Admit a rule or pointer only when a fresh agent needs it before deciding how to work; omission predictably causes scope, authority, safety, or recurring-workflow failure; it is durable beyond the task or occupies an explicit reassessable authority slot; and concise guidance can route to a canonical source.

A recurring hazard also requires likely encounter, predictable failure of the intuitive route, material consequence, and a concise preventive warning or pointer. Keep the full correction canonical and remove the warning when implementation eliminates the hazard.

Importance alone is insufficient. Route temporary status, one-task instructions, domain detail, and ordinary completion evidence elsewhere.

### 4. Run the Lens handshake

Submit one complete package to the installed `lens` skill: current `AGENTS.md`, exact changes, baseline and routes, parity and authority evidence, all prior-guidance dispositions, historical reclassification, and material assumptions or conflicts. Include enough raw evidence for adversarial review without presenting Heartbeat's preference as fact.

Lens tests identity, necessity, placement, omissions, proportionality, expression, and human authority. It challenges context momentum, capability bias, solution eagerness, convenience, recency bias, and unexamined preference accommodation without re-adjudicating verified facts, accepted completion, or grounded goal lifecycle.

Lens is back-office advice, not a decision-maker or veto. Heartbeat owns the map: reconcile supported criticism, reject unsupported criticism, and investigate uncertainty before involving the user. Material reshaping permits one final bounded Lens check, never a loop.

If Lens is not surfaced, find the installed skill before declaring it unavailable; users may call it `Mode L` or `$lens`. If it cannot be read, report the mandatory review incomplete. Never invoke it automatically outside this repository-guidance checkpoint.

### 5. Run the Mode T landing handshake

After reconciling Lens, apply the installed `semantic-compression` skill to exact proposed `AGENTS.md` wording. The pre-compression decision set remains authoritative: Mode T may remove repetition and improve expression, but never alter a rule, route, decision, constraint, dependency, exception, or approval requirement.

Compare against that decision set and restore any loss. If meaning cannot remain concise, move detail into canonical project documentation and retain only the necessary rule or pointer in `AGENTS.md`; never land the internal audit or an avoidable wall.

### 6. Reconcile, review, and apply

Resolve evidence-backed choices and Heartbeat-versus-Lens contradictions internally. Present one concise recommendation and only choices requiring human authority, in project terms. If many questions remain, find the shared policy decision or acknowledge insufficient evidence; never turn Heartbeat into an interview. Aligned runs ask nothing; changes get one consolidated approval checkpoint under applicable change control.

For a read-only review, make no edit. After explicit approval, update repository `AGENTS.md`. Repair a documentation-index pointer only when the authorised guidance change would otherwise leave it inaccurate. Do not widen into general index maintenance or global surfaces.

## Human-facing result

Preserve every decision, constraint, dependency, and material conflict while removing repetition and internal process noise:

- **Aligned or tolerable drift:** report the classification, decisive evidence, and no-change result briefly.
- **Material drift:** show only the evidence or parity gap, handoff risk, prior-guidance dispositions, proposed entrance map, reconciled recommendation, and any real human decision.
- **Exceptional wider signal:** after the repository result is complete, privately test recurrence, causal portability, material impact, and preventability. Default to silence. Append at most one non-blocking **Cross-project candidate** only when recurring evidence shows meaningful wider impact, or one unusually conclusive incident shows high impact, and a concise reusable safeguard could prevent recurrence. State briefly why it appears portable and what it could prevent, label inference unless authorised evidence proves recurrence, and invite a separate global-guidance review. Make no global proposal or change. Do not repeat an unchanged candidate in the same working context unless its evidence materially strengthens or the user asks.
- **Applied change:** show the exact change and required validation or recovery evidence.

These are coverage responsibilities, not fixed headings or an output count. Keep deeper audit evidence available on request.

## Validation

Confirm that:

- every reference exists, and current authority and intent are discoverable without loading full history;
- specialist routes and recurring hazards remain discoverable;
- displaced procedures are not current, history remains intact, and documentation-to-implementation gaps and authority limits are explicit;
- material Lens findings were resolved and only genuine human decisions surfaced;
- proposed `AGENTS.md` wording passed Mode T without changing the authoritative decision set;
- the update is proportional;
- a fresh-agent simulation finds current authority and a representative specialist route; and
- an unchanged rerun produces the same material result without another correction.

Run appropriate formatting checks and show exact change and recovery evidence where governing change control requires it.

Heartbeat's meaning is ready to freeze when more examples no longer change its purpose, boundaries, decision questions, evidence roles, gates, or observable proof. After that, revise it only when real use exposes a behavioural failure—not to accumulate examples or preferred phrasing.

Heartbeat succeeds when a fresh agent can enter with a small accurate baseline, find deeper context on demand, recognise current authority, and avoid legacy drift and predictable repository traps.
