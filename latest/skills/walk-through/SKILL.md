# Walk-through

**Version:** 0.1 · **Updated:** 13 November 2026

## Responsibility

Restore project-context understanding through recognition: how the work began, what changed the direction, what was actually established, and where it now stands. Own the recipe so the person need not remember a sequence of modes or tools.

Recover broadly in private, then curate the explanation. In voice mode the spoken walk-through is primary and the richer written reconstruction grounds it. Without voice mode, the written reconstruction is the single Walk-through surface.

## Recover enough of the work

Begin with the available conversation and relevant returned findings, decisions, experiments, reversals, decisive proofs, and remaining boundaries. Consult further accessible records only within existing task authority; a request to remember does not authorise inspecting unrelated projects, retrieving unrelated chats, or changing anything.

Do not treat a recent conclusion list or earlier compressed answer as the whole source. Check whether the available material explains the original intent, meaningful alternatives and why they changed, concrete turning points, proof and its limits, and the current position. Recover missing context from already available material before asking the person to repeat it. If a consequential gap cannot be recovered, identify it specifically and bound the reconstruction rather than inventing continuity.

Do not publish the raw discovery pool, an exhaustive transcript, tool log, or read-only audit dump. Source breadth is preparation, not the output format.

## Build the recognition route

Select the events that let this person recognise the work: a failed experiment, unexpected result, rejected route, decision pivot, or proof that changed what was believed possible. Small concrete events can matter more than abstract conclusions. Preserve their causal connections, relevant qualifications, and meaningful unresolved alternatives.

Use a coherent causal order rather than every timestamp or command. Distinguish what the source says, what was directly observed, reported evidence, and inference; do not manufacture a rationale to make the story flow. Preserve differences between proposed, attempted, observed, implemented, validated, and still unknown. Historical evidence does not automatically establish current live state.

## Make the written reconstruction inspectable

Create a curated, readable reconstruction of the agreed work, with headings or landmarks that expose the arc. Retain richer supporting details, relevant evidence references, qualifications, and the remaining boundary. Explain why a turning point mattered rather than merely naming the final decision. Completeness is coverage of that arc and its recognition-critical content, not reproduction of every source line.

Keep exact paths, code, configuration, identifiers, and other reference-level details visible where useful and safe. Do not reproduce secrets or private material outside its approved context. Use the existing visible chat or requested artifact surface; saving a file, creating a document destination, or changing project records requires corresponding authority.

## Match the actual communication mode

**Without voice:** deliver the written reconstruction alone. Do not append a second narration script or pretend a spoken tour occurred.

**With voice active:** deliver a selective top-surface spoken walk-through while providing the corresponding written grounding. Cue recognition-critical landmarks, explain their causal links and significance, and carry qualifications needed to understand what was and was not established. Every recognition-critical landmark and causal link must survive selection. Reference-level details may remain visual when speech explains their significance and preserves the route through the work.

Selectivity is not a lossy conclusion-only summary or a skeletal cover note; nor is the spoken surface the entire detailed storyline, an audiobook, or a second full rendering of the document. Use conversational explanation and identifiable section cues instead of reading technical strings, paths, code, identifiers, or citations aloud. Explain the technical relationship or proof in ordinary speech rather than removing it.

Keep both surfaces aligned in causal order, landmarks, claims, and evidence boundaries without requiring verbatim duplication. There is no universal written-first delivery rule: use an existing reconstruction or prepare the surfaces together as the interaction permits. Ensure the relevant visual grounding is available when referring to it; do not claim it is displayed or delivered when it is not. After an interruption, resume from an identifiable landmark. Reflect substantive corrections in both surfaces.

Use only the host's available, authorised communication capability. Do not start a new audio service, generate an audio file, or change settings merely to perform this skill. If spoken delivery is unavailable, explain that limit and supply the written surface; a text rehearsal is not evidence of actual speech.

## Compose without losing the outcome

Choose supporting capabilities as needed, not a compulsory pipeline. Semantic Compression may tighten a sufficiently rich foundation, but it is optional and subordinate: it cannot establish source breadth. Thin input plus faithful compression is still a thin result. If a proposed sub-operation would erase the breadth, recognition landmarks, or causal links the request needs, change the composition rather than return a technically correct but unhelpful result.

Use navigation or formatting treatments only where they improve the selected surface. Do not require another named skill, a particular provider, or a presentation framework for the recipe to work. Respect explicit output constraints; surface a material conflict rather than silently overriding the person's request.

Before handing back, check that the written surface reconstructs the work rather than dumps it; a voice run preserves the recognition route without reciting all supporting detail; and both retain the evidence states and remaining boundary.

## Authority boundary

Explain existing work; do not resume a goal, execute the underlying workflow, redesign the project, or change its records merely because the reconstruction exposes an opportunity. Existing project authority and operating safeguards still govern. This is conversational context restoration, not clinical memory support, a persistent memory store, or proof that the person has remembered or accepted anything.
